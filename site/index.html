A day with 2poke

It keeps its own little routine.

Between your questions, the bear lives its life. It picks what to do by the time of day, and some activities come with a little room of their own.

Talk

Ask in plain words. It asks before it acts.

Click the bear on your desktop and type. The answer streams into its speech bubble while it talks. It can also work on your Mac: run commands, sort files, play music and set reminders.

Senses

It notices what’s going on.

The bear reacts to your desktop the way a pet reacts to a room, without reading what’s on your screen.

  • Music

    When Music or Spotify is playing, it bobs its head along.

  • Window edges

    Drop it on top of a window and it sits on the edge with its legs dangling.

  • Your cursor

    It watches your cursor, flinches if you swoop at it, and giggles if you wiggle over it.

  • Being picked up

    Pick it up and it dangles. Swing it around for too long and it gets dizzy.

  • When you step away

    It taps on the glass to see where you went, then curls up for a nap.

  • Waiting on you

    When it needs your OK, it taps its foot and checks its tiny watch.

Rooms

Make it feel at home.

Pick a floor for it to stand on, then give it something to do. Some activities bring a little room along: a window nook for the laptop, a tea room for tea, a fireside library for reading.

Privacy and manners

Friendly, never nosy.

A desk buddy should feel like company. 2poke follows these rules out of the box.

  • 01

    It asks first.

    Commands and file changes can wait for your OK. Choose how often: every time, only when unsure, or full access, where deny rules still deny and risky commands still ask.

  • 02

    Keys stay in your Keychain.

    Your AI account keys and sign-in tokens live in the macOS Keychain. They’re never written to a file or handed to a command.

  • 03

    Your cursor stays between you two.

    It uses where your cursor is to react in the moment. That position is never logged, stored or sent to the AI.

  • 04

    It only sees window edges.

    To sit on a window it reads where windows are. It never reads their names or what’s inside them.

  • 05

    Four check-ins an hour, at most.

    It speaks up on its own only a few times an hour, and never during quiet hours or Do not disturb. It can hide over fullscreen apps, too.

  • 06

    Light as a feather.

    The bear is a native Mac panel drawn in Rust. At rest it uses about 0.3% of your CPU.

Studio

Bring your own character.

The bear is the first mascot, and you can make more. Draw yours in Aseprite, import it, and set up its clips, behaviors and idle moves in the built-in studio.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Poke the bear. It won’t mind.

When can I get 2poke?

Soon. We’re finishing the last pieces now. Join the waitlist and we’ll send you one email when it’s ready to download.

Does it work on Windows?

2poke is made for macOS first. Windows is planned for later.

Which AI does it use?

Claude by default. You can sign in with another provider’s account or API key and switch models any time from the menu bar.

Will it slow down my Mac?

It shouldn’t. The bear is a small native panel, and at rest it uses about 0.3% of the CPU. You can also hide it over fullscreen apps.

Can it really do things on my computer?

Yes, if you let it. It can run commands, read and edit files, control Music or Spotify, and open a small browser window. You choose how often it asks first. Even with full access, risky commands and sensitive files still ask.

Will it interrupt me?

Rarely. It speaks up on its own at most four times an hour and stays quiet during quiet hours and Do not disturb.

Where does it live on my Mac?

In the menu bar, with no Dock icon. The bear floats above your windows, and you can move it to another display from its menu.

Can I make my own mascot?

Yes. The built-in studio imports Aseprite files, lets you edit clips, behaviors and idle moves, and checks everything before it goes on your desktop.
